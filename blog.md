# How to Optimize Blog Posts for SEO: A 2025 Checklist

Before publishing your next blog post, make sure it ticks these boxes:

- ✅ Target keyword in H1
- ✅ Engaging meta description
- ✅ Keyword in first 100 words
- ✅ At least one internal and external link
- ✅ Use of bullet points or numbered lists
- ✅ Mobile responsive layout
- ✅ Image compression + alt text

SEO is in the details — don’t skip the basics!
