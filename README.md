# SEO Blog Case Study – "How to Do Keyword Research for Free: A Beginner’s Guide"

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study documents the creation of an SEO-optimized blog article titled **“How to Do Keyword Research for Free: A Beginner’s Guide.”** It is designed for digital marketing beginners who want to understand keyword research fundamentals and apply them using free tools.

---

## 🎯 Goals

- Educate beginners about free keyword research tools
- Structure content for SEO ranking
- Showcase practical examples with screenshots
